The Lighthouse Keeper's Daughter

Chapter 1

The storm arrived before Mira finished counting the boats. She stood at the
edge of the rocks, salt spray needling her cheeks, and watched the horizon
fold itself into grey. Her father had warned her about nights like this —
nights when the sea forgot the difference between water and sky.

"Get to the tower," he had said, "and don't come down until the light holds
steady for an hour." She had laughed at him then. She was not laughing now.

Marcus: The wind's picking up fast. We should have left an hour ago.

Elena: There wasn't time. You know that.

Mira climbed the spiral stairs two at a time, her lungs burning by the
time she reached the lamp room. The great lens sat cold and dark, waiting.

Chapter 2

By morning the storm had passed, leaving the harbor scattered with kelp
and the wrecked hull of a fishing boat nobody recognized. Mira walked the
tideline, half-expecting to find something else the sea had given up.

She found a girl instead — soaked, shivering, and very much alive.

Elena: Where... where am I?

Marcus: Safe. You're safe now.

Mira wrapped her jacket around the stranger's shoulders and helped her
toward the lighthouse, already composing the story she'd tell her father.
